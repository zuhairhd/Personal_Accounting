Overview Double-Entry Accounts Opening Balances Transactions Credit Cards Loans Reports Month-End Reset & Backup

This is a reusable personal finance accounting template. It starts completely empty — no transactions, no personal data, no imported balances. Anyone can use it to track:

  • Bank accounts and wallet cash
  • Credit card balances and payments
  • Income (salary, freelance, rental, etc.)
  • Expenses (food, rent, utilities, education, etc.)
  • Loans given and received
  • Net worth over time
This template contains no private data. It uses a built-in default chart of accounts only. All data you enter stays in your local database file.

Every transaction affects at least two accounts. Total Debit must always equal Total Credit.

Trial Balance

Lists all accounts with non-zero balances. Total Debit must equal Total Credit. Shows Balanced ✓ when correct.

General Ledger

Select any account to see all its transactions with a running balance. Good for tracing the history of a bank account or expense.

Income & Expense Statement

Shows all income accounts and expense accounts. Bottom line is Surplus (income > expenses) or Deficit (expenses > income).

Net Worth Statement (Balance Sheet)

Shows Assets, Liabilities, and Equity. Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Liabilities. Positive = you own more than you owe.

Expenses by Category

Ranks all expense accounts by amount with percentage of total. Shows where your money goes.

Transactions by Date

All entries in chronological order grouped by day. Useful for reviewing a specific week or month.

  1. Enter all transactions for the month.
  2. Check Trial Balance — must show Balanced ✓.
  3. Review Expenses by Category — identify overspending.
  4. Check Income & Expense Statement — confirm surplus or deficit.
  5. Reconcile each bank account via Bank Reconciliation.
  6. Review Net Worth Statement — is net worth improving?
  7. Export reports to CSV and save for your records.
  8. Back up personal_template.db.

Backup

All your data is in personal_template.db. Copy this file to a safe location regularly:

copy C:\dev\Personal_Accounting_Template\personal_template.db C:\Backup\personal_template_backup.db
Reset to Default Template

Go to Admin → Reset Data in the sidebar. This deletes all transactions, people, reconciliations, and accounts, then restores the default 50-account chart of accounts. Type RESET to confirm.

Reset is permanent. Always back up your database before resetting.
